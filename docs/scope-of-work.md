# Scope of Work

## Objective

Build a modern corporate intranet platform focused on communication, visibility, engagement, culture, and knowledge sharing.

The scope intentionally excludes transactional HR processes such as attendance, leave management, payroll, and recruitment workflows.

---

## Included Features

### Communication

* Leadership messages
* Company announcements
* Business updates
* Leadership outcomes

### Visibility

* Department impact
* Strategic priorities
* Project wins
* Organizational milestones

### Engagement

* Events
* Community discussions
* Employee stories
* Celebration feeds

### Recognition

* Peer appreciation
* Achievement spotlight
* Hall of fame
* Leaderboards

### Knowledge

* Policies
* Handbooks
* Templates
* Learning resources

### Media

* Image gallery
* Video gallery
* Event albums
